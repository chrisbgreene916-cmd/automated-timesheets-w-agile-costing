How it works

  • Story, Epic, and Program cards are created and maintained in Jira as the normal source of truth.
  • TaskTop mirrors the hierarchy into Planview in real time — a Jira Program becomes a Planview Project, and an Epic becomes a Phase.
  • Each day, TaskTop reads how many cards are open per assignee, and which Program/Phase each rolls up to.
  • 8 hours is split evenly across a person's open cards' parent Programs — 2 open cards under 2 Programs means 4 hrs logged to each.
  • Hours post automatically to each assignee's Planview timesheet — no manual entry required.

Tech stack

🗂️ Jira 🔁 TaskTop 📋 Planview AgilePlace ⚙️ Hierarchy mapping 🕒 Daily time post

What makes it distinctive

  • Timesheet accuracy stops depending on people remembering to log hours at all.
  • Time reflects actual delivery status in Jira — not a guess reconstructed at week's end.
  • TaskTop keeps Jira and Planview in sync automatically, so the two systems never drift apart.
  • Finance/PMO get consistent, hierarchy-accurate project hours daily — not just at month-end.
